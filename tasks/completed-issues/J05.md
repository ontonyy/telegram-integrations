# J05 — Route Telegram Business support to an operator

[Русская версия](../../ru/tasks/completed-issues/J05.md) · [GitHub issue #5](https://github.com/ontonyy/telegram-integrations/issues/5)

## What was delivered

The Spring sample models Business connection updates, incoming/edit/delete message events, a minimal support ticket, a human takeover, and a guarded reply through a connected Business bot.

## How it works

`BusinessSupportService` reads the selected connection rights and keeps synthetic ticket metadata without retaining message text. An operator must pass the application's allowlist and ticket assignment checks. A reply is sent with `business_connection_id` only while the connection is active, `can_reply` is available, and Telegram's reply-window condition is satisfied. Otherwise the sample returns a manual-reply instruction.

## Use this in a real project

Connect a Business-enabled bot to the company's Telegram Business account, choose only the intended private-chat recipients and rights, then connect a properly authenticated operator UI or helpdesk. Use durable ticket storage, assignment checks, audit, and retention/deletion rules. Do not treat the connection as access to groups or an entire account archive.
